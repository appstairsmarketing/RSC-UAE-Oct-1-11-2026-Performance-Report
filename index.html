Recommendations

1. Google Ads is paused as of Oct 12, at the client's request.

2. We need new, real doctor content. More new videos with the doctors, more doctor presence across our ads, and more patient testimonials are needed — recently we have relied almost entirely on AI-generated content.

3. Run Eyelid ads on Snapchat. With Google Ads paused, we can take part of the Google Ads budget and use it to launch Eyelid ads on Snapchat, where LASIK lead generation is already performing well.
